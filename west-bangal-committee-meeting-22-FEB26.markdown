    <p>
    The <strong>14th West Bengal Zone Conference of AIIBSCSTEWC</strong> was
    successfully organized today at the prestigious
    <strong>Kolkata Mulali Youth Center</strong>. The conference witnessed
    enthusiastic participation from members, office bearers, and delegates
    from various branches across the West Bengal zone. The event reflected
    the growing strength, unity, and commitment of the
    <strong>All India Indian Bank SC/ST Employees’ Welfare Council</strong>.
  </p>
   <p>
    The venue was filled with energy and purpose as members gathered to
    review organizational progress, discuss key welfare issues, and chart
    the future roadmap for the council in the region. The conference served
    as an important platform for strengthening coordination among members
    and reinforcing the council’s mission of employee welfare and social
    empowerment.
  </p>
    <h2 class="h4 mt-4">Election of New West Bengal Committee</h2>
    <p>
      A major highlight of the conference was the smooth and democratic
      election of the new West Bengal Zone body. Members actively participated
      in the process, ensuring transparency and collective decision-making.
      The newly elected team received warm congratulations from all attendees.
    </p>

    grassroots coordination.
