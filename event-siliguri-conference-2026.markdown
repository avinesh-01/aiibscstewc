 <article class="article-content">
 <p class="lead">It gives us immense pleasure and pride to announce the <strong>successful completion of the Siliguri Zonal Conference 2026</strong>  and the formation of the <strong>Siliguri Zonal Unit of the All India Indian Bank SC/ST Employees’ Welfare Council (AIIBSCSTEWC)</strong>.</p>
  <p> The successful organization of this important conference marks a  significant milestone in the continued growth and strengthening of our Council. The conference brought together members, office bearers, representatives, and well-wishers with a shared commitment towards <strong>unity, welfare, equality, representation, and organizational development</strong>.</p>
  <p>The formation of the <strong>Siliguri Zonal Unit</strong> is an important step towards expanding and strengthening the organizational structure of AIIBSCSTEWC. It provides a stronger platform for members to come together,  discuss issues affecting employees, share their experiences, raise concerns, and work collectively towards finding constructive solutions.</p>
  <p> The conference also provided an opportunity to reaffirm our collective commitment to the <strong>welfare, dignity, rights, professional development, and social empowerment of SC/ST employees of Indian Bank</strong>. </p>
 <p> A strong and united organizational structure is essential to ensure that  the voice of every member is heard and that issues concerning employee welfare, equality, representation, and social justice receive appropriate attention.</p>
    <p>We sincerely congratulate and extend our heartfelt appreciation to <strong>all members, office bearers, delegates, organizers, and well-wishers</strong> whose valuable support, participation, dedication, and cooperation contributed to the successful conduct of the Siliguri  Zonal Unit  Conference and the establishment of the new Zonal Unit.</p>
    <p>The formation of this Zonal Unit is not merely an organizational achievement; it represents another step forward in our collective journey towards <strong>greater unity, stronger representation, mutual support, and inclusive growth</strong>.</p>
    <p>We are confident that the Siliguri Zonal Unit will continue to work with  dedication and commitment for the welfare of its members and in furtherance of the objectives of AIIBSCSTEWC.</p>
    <p>As we move forward, let us continue to strengthen our organization, encourage greater participation, support one another, and work together with determination and a shared vision for a better and more equitable future.</p>
