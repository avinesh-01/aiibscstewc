            <h1 class="fw-bold mb-4">AIIBSCSTEWC Participates in Parliamentary Committee Study Visit on SC/ST Welfare</h1>
            <p class="text-muted"> Published by AIIBSCSTEWC </p>
            <!-- ARTICLE CONTENT -->
            <p>
                The All India Indian Bank SC/ST Employees' Welfare Council (AIIBSCSTEWC)
                continues its dedicated efforts toward protecting the rights,
                dignity, and welfare of Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe employees.
                Guided by the principles of equality, inclusion, and social justice,
                the Council has consistently worked to ensure that employee concerns
                are addressed and opportunities for professional growth are promoted.
            </p>
            <p>
                As part of these ongoing efforts, representatives of AIIBSCSTEWC
                participated in the Study Visit of the Parliamentary Committee on
                the Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. The visit
                served as an important platform for reviewing welfare initiatives,
                understanding challenges faced by employees, and discussing measures
                aimed at strengthening representation and inclusion within institutions.
            </p>
            <p>
                The Parliamentary Committee plays a significant role in monitoring
                the implementation of policies and welfare schemes intended for
                Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. Such visits help assess
                progress, identify areas for improvement, and encourage institutions
                to adopt best practices that support equal opportunities and employee
                empowerment.
            </p>
            <p>
                During the visit, discussions focused on welfare measures,
                representation, career advancement opportunities, workplace inclusion,
                and the effective implementation of constitutional safeguards and
                government policies. The interaction reinforced the importance of
                collaboration between policymakers, institutions, and employee
                welfare organizations in achieving meaningful progress.
            </p>
            <p>
                AIIBSCSTEWC remains committed to supporting initiatives that foster
                employee welfare, dignity, and professional development. The Council
                believes that strong institutions are built through transparency,
                accountability, and equal opportunity for all employees.
            </p>
            <p>
                The Council expresses its sincere appreciation to all members who
                continue to support its mission and objectives. Their trust,
                dedication, and unity have enabled AIIBSCSTEWC to effectively
                represent employee interests and contribute to positive change.
            </p>
            <p>
                Participation in the Parliamentary Committee Study Visit represents
                another important milestone in the Council's journey toward
                strengthening employee welfare and promoting social justice.
                AIIBSCSTEWC will continue to work tirelessly alongside stakeholders,
                policymakers, and institutional leadership to create a more inclusive,
                equitable, and empowering work environment.
            </p>
