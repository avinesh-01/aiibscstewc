    <h4 class="fw-bold mb-3">Dear Brothers and Sisters,</h4>
    <p>   The birthday of our beloved Babasaheb, <strong> Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar</strong>, is celebrated across the country as <strong>Dr. Ambedkar Jayanti</strong>. For all of us, this is not just a date—it is a day of pride, respect, and inspiration.</p>
    <p>Every year, our state and zonal units celebrate this occasion with great enthusiasm. Whether in small towns or big cities, the feeling remains the same—deep respect for Babasaheb and a strong belief in equality and justice.</p>
    <hr class="my-4">
    <h5 class="fw-bold">🏛️ Celebration in Delhi</h5>
    <p> The celebration takes a grand form. Parliament Street becomes a vibrant hub where organizations set up stalls and welcome people from across the country.</p>
    <p>Lakhs of our brothers and sisters gather to celebrate this special day. The atmosphere is filled with energy, devotion, and unity. </p>
    <hr class="my-4">
    <h5 class="fw-bold"> Contribution of AIIBSCSTEWC Delhi Unit </h5>
    <p>Every year, on behalf of our Council, our Delhi Unit sets up a stall at the venue. This has become a proud tradition for us.</p>
    <p>During the celebration, we organize food distribution for thousands of people. It is our small effort to serve the community and share warmth and respect with everyone who joins the celebration. </p>
    <p>More than anything, it is about connecting with our brothers and sisters,  meeting people from different parts of the country, and strengthening  our bond as a community.</p>
    <hr class="my-4">
    <h5 class="fw-bold">❤️ Spirit of the Day</h5>
    <p>What makes this day truly special is the feeling behind it. People come together with respect, kindness, and a sense of belonging. </p>
    <p>Serving others and being part of this celebration reminds us of the values   Babasaheb stood for—equality, dignity, and humanity.</p>
    <div class="text-center mt-4"><p class="fst-italic mb-2">"Educate, Agitate, Organize"</p><h4 class="fw-bold text-primary mb-0">Jai Bhim!</h4></div>
