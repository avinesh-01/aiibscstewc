Notice to Central Executive Committee (CEC) Members

Dear CEC Member,

You are well aware that the Hon’ble High Court at Calcutta, vide its Order dated 10.07.2025 in FMAT 261 of 2025 with FMA 857 of 2025, appointed two Joint Administrators to conduct the election of the General Body and the new Central Executive Committee (CEC) of All India Indian Bank SC/ST Employees Welfare Council (AIIBSCSTEWC).

Meeting Held on 22.11.2025 – Kolkata

Pursuant to the above order, a physical meeting was held at Kolkata on 22.11.2025 between the Joint Administrators, the President, and the Election Commissioner. It was finalized that the Rules & Regulations for the Election must be drafted and hosted on the official website of the Council.

Draft Rules & Regulations – 17th National Conference / GBM

The draft Rules and Regulations for the 17th National Conference / General Body Meeting and Election of the new Central Executive Committee have been prepared strictly in accordance with the Council By-laws, West Bengal Societies Registration Act, 1961 and Rules, 1963.

Action Required:
All CEC Members are requested to read the draft Rules & Regulations carefully and provide their consent in the prescribed format along with their preference of venue for conducting the GBM.

Corrections / Suggestions

If any member wishes to suggest corrections to the draft rules, the same may be sent within 7 days from the receipt of this notice. Failing which, it shall be construed that consent has been given.
